Quality gasoline sourced from independent refineries, sold at a fair price right on Moraga Road. Every gallon meets California's strict clean-fuel standards.

Where our fuel comes from

Independent refineries.
No shortcuts on quality.

Moraga Gas sources its gasoline from independent refineries instead of a big-brand chain. Skipping the brand-name markup lets us keep prices fair for the neighborhood.

What doesn't change is the standard. Every gallon we pump is blended to meet the same state and federal requirements as fuel sold at any station in California.

01 · CARB

California clean-burning fuel

All of our gasoline is California reformulated gasoline, made to meet the Air Resources Board's rules, some of the strictest in the nation.

02 · Detergents

Cleaning additives included

Our fuel contains detergent additives that help keep fuel injectors and intake valves free of deposits.

03 · Inspected

State-checked pumps

California weights and measures officials inspect pumps for accurate measuring and test fuel samples for quality.

04 · Value

Fair price, full quality

Without a big brand's markup, more of what you spend goes into your tank, not into a logo.
